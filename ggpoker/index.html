Import your spots from GGPoker

Under GGPoker’s rules, SpotShot never touches its client. Pick one of two ways to get the pictures, then import them.

Option 1: your screenshots, during play

  1. Press Win+Shift+S and pick Window mode on the top bar (the third button). It stays chosen.

  2. Click the table. The screenshot is that window alone.

  3. In the Snipping Tool app, open Settings (the … menu) and turn on Automatically save screenshots. They go to PicturesScreenshots.

  4. Optional: in the same settings, let Print Screen open the Snipping Tool, or map Ctrl+4 (SpotShot's own key) to Win+Shift+S in PowerToys Keyboard Manager.

  5. In SpotShot Settings, set Screenshots folder to PicturesScreenshots. SpotShot offers the new ones at every start.

  • Table: once, at your decision.
  • Lobby: once per tournament, on the prize list. First resize the lobby window until every prize row shows. If they still do not fit, scroll and shoot again.

Turn off Screen Guard in GGPoker (Game Settings, Client Security), or the screenshots come out black.

Option 2: PokerCraft, after play

  1. Open my.pokercraft.com, My Tournaments.

  2. Tick the tournament.

  3. Open Game History and click the hand.

  4. In the hand viewer, in chips view, press the download icon (first in the header).

  5. Open Tournament Info and press its download icon.

Take both from the same tournament: SpotShot pairs them by name.

Import and analyse

  1. Close every poker client.

  2. Press Import screenshots, or drop the files on SpotShot.

  3. In Raw captures, tick the ones you want.

  4. Press Analyse.

Find it in the library

Each picture becomes a spot in All spots, newest first.

A lobby alone gives a Structure only spot.

Open a spot: Copy hand for a solver, or Export to HRC.

Not only GGPoker

The import works for every room SpotShot supports.
