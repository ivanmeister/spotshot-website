Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when SpotShot and the website spotshot.gg are used, for what purposes, and by whom. SpotShot does not require an account and contains no analytics or advertising.

About the Developer

SpotShot (the “Software”) and the website spotshot.gg (the “Website”) are developed and operated by Ivan Meister (the “Developer”). References to “you” are to any person who uses the Software or the Website. Questions regarding this document may be addressed to the Developer at spotshot.gg@gmail.com.

Data stored on your computer

All data created by the Software, including captures, the spot library, hand setups, tournament structures, notes, settings and logs, is stored exclusively on your computer. The Developer holds no copy of this data and has no means of accessing it. You may delete it at any time by deleting the files or uninstalling the Software.

Connections made by the Software

When the Software starts, and periodically while it is running, it connects to the Developer’s servers in order to verify that the installed copy is valid and to check for updates. It also downloads updates when you choose to install them. These connections do not transmit any personal information or any content from your captures or library.

IP addresses

As with any connection over the internet, these connections and visits to the Website reveal your IP address to the hosting providers that serve them. These providers may record IP addresses and basic request details, such as the time of a request, in their technical logs for the purpose of operating and securing their services.

The hosting providers have access to these IP addresses; the Developer does not. Neither the Software nor the Developer’s servers record IP addresses, and the Developer does not receive, store or view them.

The Website

The Website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking technologies and does not load content from third-party websites. Your browser stores the theme and language you select on your device only.

Contacting the Developer and sending logs

If you contact the Developer via Discord or by email, the Developer receives the information you provide, such as your username and the content of your message.

A log exported from the Software is saved as a text file on your computer and is transmitted to the Developer only if you choose to send it. It contains technical information about the Software, your computer and its settings, and recent activity and errors. Before the file is saved, the Software removes player nicknames, text read from poker tables, the titles of other windows, and your Windows user and computer names. You may review the contents of the file before sending it. Messages and logs are used solely to respond to your enquiry and to resolve technical issues.

Machine ID

The Software displays a machine ID in its settings: an identifier that distinguishes your computer from others. It is not transmitted automatically. If you provide it to the Developer, for example to obtain access or a key, it is used solely for that purpose. If special access is granted to your copy of the Software, its machine ID is kept on the Developer’s access list.

Disclosure of data

The Developer does not sell personal data or share it for advertising purposes. The Developer uses service providers to host the Website, distribute downloads and updates, and operate the servers to which the Software connects. These providers process IP addresses and request details in their own systems and may do so in countries other than your own, including the United States. The Developer discloses information to public authorities only where required by law.

Retention

  • Technical logs: retained by the service providers for a limited period in accordance with their own policies.
  • Logs sent to the Developer: retained until the issue is resolved, then deleted.
  • Messages: retained in the conversation in which they were sent until deleted by you or by the Developer.
  • Machine IDs granted special access: retained for as long as that access remains in effect.

Legal bases

Data is processed where necessary to provide the Software in accordance with the Terms of Use, on the basis of the Developer’s legitimate interest in keeping the Software functional, secure and up to date, and on the basis of your decision to send a message or a log. This applies under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and, where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Your rights

You may request confirmation of whether the Developer holds personal data about you, request access to, correction or deletion of such data, or object to its processing, by writing to spotshot.gg@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or with the data protection authority of your country of residence.

Age

The Software is intended for adults who are legally permitted to play poker in their country of residence. It is not intended for persons under 18 years of age.

Third-party websites and services

Poker rooms, Discord and other websites linked from the Website are governed by their own privacy policies. This Privacy Policy does not apply to them.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

The Developer may amend this Privacy Policy. Any changes will be published on this page together with the date of the update.
