Questions and answers

How SpotShot works, what it saves and how to install it.

For example: HRC, anonymous, SmartScreen

28 questions

Basics

What is SpotShot?

An app for saving your preflop MTT spots with ease. One key captures the table, opens the tournament lobby and combines the two screenshots into one capture. After your session, each capture becomes a spot in your library. Each spot comes with the hand setup and tournament structure files, ready for ICM calculators such as HRC or GTO Wizard.

How does SpotShot work?

Press your shortcut at the table during your session: SpotShot opens the tournament lobby, takes the screenshots and saves them as one picture. If the prizes don't all fit in one screenshot, the lobby window is enlarged or the prize list is scrolled and captured automatically. In mystery bounty tournaments, during the mystery stage, SpotShot also makes extra clicks on the table to capture the current chests on GGNetwork, WPT Network and QQPK.

After your session, once every poker room is closed, the spot library becomes accessible and you can analyse your captures: with the Play button on the main window or the Analyse button in the library. Each capture becomes a spot of its own in the library, with a name, the hand setup and the tournament structure files.

To have the spot analysis start by itself after a session, turn on Analyse after every session in the Raw captures queue's menu in the spot library.

What is SpotShot for?

To save a preflop spot quickly with zero hassle, no taking your eyes off the tables during your session. Capturing the lobby automatically keeps the current information for the spot that the table does not always show: the actual current prizes (if late registration is still open), the starting stack and the chests still left in mystery bounty tournaments.

Using SpotShot

Can SpotShot take normal screenshots?

Yes. A second shortcut works like the Snipping Tool: click the window you want, or select part of the screen. The screenshot is copied to your clipboard straight away.

How do I use SpotShot?

When the preflop action reaches you, press your table capture shortcut and click on the table.

To skip clicking on the table, you can turn on Capture without clicking in Settings. Then you just hover the table and press the shortcut to capture it.

After your session, start the analysis from the main window or the library.

Can I send my raw captures to someone else?

Yes. A raw capture is saved as a single picture that you can send as it is, and it carries everything needed to analyse it. To export several, use Export as a zip in the queue's menu. The other person can import them into their library with Import raw captures.

Screenshots that were edited, compressed by a messenger or not taken with SpotShot cannot be imported.

I analysed a spot with the wrong settings. Can I do it again?

Yes. Right-click the spot in the library and choose Back to raw captures. The spot goes back to the table screenshots it was made from and disappears from the library. You can turn those screenshots into a spot again from the queue, with the settings you need.

How do I hide my nickname or every nickname at the table?

Hiding nicknames is set in the queue's menu in the library: press the three dots to choose the setting for every spot at once.

Hand setup and ICM calculators

Do I still need Hand2Note to find the hand?

No. SpotShot builds the hand from your capture, so you no longer find it in a tracker and export it. Click Copy hand in the library, then paste it into HRC or GTO Wizard. The file is also saved in the spot’s folder.

You can keep your tracker for stats.

Where does the spot library keep my spots?

In the Library folder on your Desktop. You can move it with Library folder… in Settings.

Captures waiting to be analysed are in its Raw captures folder. Each spot is a normal folder you can open in File Explorer, with its stars, tags and notes inside. Nothing is uploaded.

What does SpotShot save besides the picture?

Two files, next to the picture in the spot’s folder: the hand setup and the tournament structure. The original capture is kept too, so you can analyse the spot again.

The hand setup is a PokerStars-style hand history that stops at your decision. The tournament structure lists what each place pays, taken from the lobby in your capture.

Does the hand setup record nicknames?

No. You are Hero, and everyone else is named by position: UTG, CO, BTN and so on.

Which programs can import the files?

HRC (Holdem Resource Calculator) and GTO Wizard both import the hand setup. HRC also loads the tournament structure, the same way it loads a SharkScope export.

Do I still need SharkScope or HRC Ninja for the structure?

No. SpotShot takes the payouts from the lobby in your capture and saves them as a structure file. Load it into HRC as it is.

What if SpotShot cannot analyse the spot?

If part of the table cannot be read, you still get the spot. Its hand setup has what could be read, such as the seats, stacks and button, so it still imports. Check the positions after importing.

If a capture cannot be analysed at all, it stays in the queue with the reason. You can try it again from there. If a setting was wrong, right-click the spot, choose Back to raw captures and analyse it again.

Download and install

What does SpotShot cost?

Nothing. SpotShot is in a free public beta with every feature included.

I found a bug. What should I do?

SpotShot is in beta, so bugs happen. Export a log in Settings › Help and send it to us on Discord. Tell us what you were doing.

The log leaves out player nicknames.

Which versions of Windows does SpotShot run on?

Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit. The installer includes everything SpotShot needs.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is the download safe?

Yes. Windows warns about any program from a publisher it does not know. Signing as a publisher costs money we would rather spend on SpotShot itself.

Click More info, then Run anyway. You only need to do this once, when you first install it.

The lobby does not open when I capture. What should I check?

Your poker client, HUD or table manager may be running as administrator. Windows then blocks clicks from other programs, so run SpotShot as administrator too.

If it still happens, export a log in Settings › Help and send it to us on Discord.

Rules and privacy

Can I use SpotShot while I play?

Yes. While a poker client is open, SpotShot only opens the lobby and takes screenshots. Everything to do with analysing the screenshots is only possible once every poker client is closed.

SpotShot follows the rules of every room it supports and gives no advantage at the table. We consulted the poker rooms on this.

Is SpotShot safe in terms of privacy?

Yes. Your captures, spots and notes stay on your PC, and nobody can see them, including us.

SpotShot only goes online to check for updates and to confirm periodically that your personal version is current. It never sends anything about you, your PC or your captures. You can keep an eye on this on the Network tab in Task Manager.

Can scammers get access to my PC through SpotShot?

No. SpotShot developers have closed the vulnerabilities that third parties could exploit to get access to your PC. The only thing SpotShot can download is a new update, which is checked before it installs to make sure it matches the version we released. SpotShot's architecture is built around security and making as few online requests as possible. SpotShot was made as an offline-first app, with the greatest focus on privacy and on protecting your personal data.

Always download SpotShot only from the official source, spotshot.gg.

What is in the log I export?

The SpotShot version, your settings, the screenshots taken and the reasons for any failures, and the list of last actions SpotShot took.

It leaves out player nicknames, text from your tables, other windows' titles and your Windows user name. The file is saved to your Desktop and goes nowhere unless you send it.

Why won't the library open?

The library is unavailable while any poker client is running, even minimised or in the tray. When one of the rooms is opened, the library becomes unavailable and closes by itself, keeping everything you were working on. Spot analysis happens only outside your playing sessions.

Updates and compatibility

What are updates for? Do I need them?

Yes. SpotShot is tuned to each room, down to the size of its table and lobby, so every update a room makes can break table captures. We try to fix it quickly, and the fix reaches you in a new update.

Does SpotShot work with table managers?

Yes, including non-standard table sizes.
