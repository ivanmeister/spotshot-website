1.0.5

Latest20 Nov 2026

Overview

A new update for SpotShot: Ocean KO bounties converted to dollars, Day 1 flights with estimated payouts, CoinPoker mystery bounties, presentations as images in one click and more reliable recognition of GGPoker hand screenshots.

  • Ocean KO on GGPoker: SpotShot now reads the sea-creature icon beside each player and converts it into an estimated knockout in dollars, and for every player listed in the lobby's Players tab it takes the exact bounty from there.
  • Day 1 flights on GGPoker and CoinPoker: a Day 1 has no prize list yet, so SpotShot now estimates the prize pool and the payouts for the whole field of all flights from the room's usual payout structure.
  • CoinPoker mystery bounties: SpotShot now opens the mystery bounty list on the table and reads the bounties left in it.

Import

  • Hand screenshots from PokerCraft and the GGPoker client are now recognized far more reliably: the blinds and the level are read correctly, replays without the yellow action bubble are recognized, hands in yuan are supported, different tournament names are read correctly, and Hero's cards are recognized even when the client tilts them or an Ocean KO sea-creature icon covers them.
  • Added import of PokerStars final table replays from KaliPoker streams: SpotShot reads the table, the results and the bounties, and when the frame does not show whose turn it is, it asks "Who is the hero?". GGMillion$ screenshots from 720p videos are now recognized too.
  • Added import of Triton Poker hand screenshots from its app and website, and "Make this player the hero" on the spot page lets you choose whose spot to study.

Presentations

  • Presentation in one click: the Create presentation button now sits beside Open in HRC in the spot's action row whenever a presentation can be made from a solved model.
  • Added the ability to save a presentation as a 4K image or copy it as an image, to send it straight to a chat without a PDF.
  • Retake from HRC on a range slot takes a new screenshot of that range. To take a different range, just rename the slot and SpotShot works out which spot you need: for example, rename "BTN vs CO RFI" to "SB vs CO RFI" and SpotShot replaces the BTN range with the SB range.
  • A presentation's preview now fills up live as the ranges arrive.
  • Ranges from HRC now fill the space under their titles and follow the order of the hand's branches, and Locked in HRC puts a lock mark beside a range's title.
  • The editor's toolbar has been redesigned with everything grouped into fewer buttons, and Open in new window lets you work on two presentations at once.

Library

  • Triton payouts import: a prize list copied from a website can now be pasted with Paste prizes in Correct the tournament, or with Ctrl+V in the library. It is linked to the open spot if that spot has no payouts yet, and otherwise becomes a new Structure only spot.
  • Anonymous mode now also hides the nicknames in a lobby's players list: in GGPoker's Players tab and staking panel, and in CoinPoker's players list.
  • Knockouts on the spot page are shown in big blinds beside the money only while late registration is open, and in money alone after it.
  • Added a Position names setting: classic UTG and EP, or numbered U9, U8 and U7, in new spot names, hand files and menus, so spots read the way you are used to.
  • After an update, SpotShot now shows a short What's new box with the version's main news and a link to the full list of changes.

Fixes

  • Optimization: windows no longer flash black while opening or resizing, Settings opens faster, and the library sorts and resizes without lag, even with thousands of spots.
  • Lobbies are read more reliably: SpotShot finds the CoinPoker lobby even when the table is titled just "CoinPoker", and PokerDom lobbies are captured more reliably too.
  • Analysing a screenshot no longer closes SpotShot in some cases.
  • The grid view again shows two sharp cards per row after switching from the list. Before, a single blurry spot card could stretch across the whole width.
  • Fixed several presentation problems: a deleted presentation now frees its name, text slots keep their line breaks in the PDF, a slot's More menu works and its handles no longer flicker. Copying a picture and dragging a slot work again.
  • Fixed cases in which an import or move error left a notice stuck on screen.
  • Other small fixes in the library, presentations and recognition.

1.0.4

11 Nov 2026

Overview

A new update for SpotShot: a noticeably cleaner interface, presentations with a drawn table for replays, one-click re-analysis of spots, reworked notes and far more reliable recognition of hands from PokerCraft and the client.

Presentations

  • Drawn table for replays: replay screenshots have no clean table at the moment of the decision, so for spots from PokerCraft and the client's replayer SpotShot now draws the table itself from Hero's seat, in its own style, in a dark or light theme and with the room's name.
  • All key decisions in a spot: ranges are now automatically transferred to the presentation for every decision up to Hero's, so complex hands get more than four of them.
  • Clear titles: each range's title shows what action the player is responding to, and Hero's decision always comes last.
  • Smart templates: the sizes of the table, slots and Bubble factors are chosen automatically, so the presentation fills the whole page and Bubble factors fit their slot exactly.
  • Putting HRC ranges into a presentation is now about twice as fast.
  • The Equal slots layout has been reworked: any slot can now be moved, and slots are arranged in two rows at most.
  • Late reg pages now have a Notes slot that can be deleted if you do not need it.
  • The Back to original button returns a presentation to how it was first created from the solved model.
  • The title size can now be changed with Ctrl+Shift+, and Ctrl+Shift+. or with the − and + buttons in the slot's right-click menu.
  • Solved HRC models saved as .hrcv now work just like .hrcz.
  • Added the ability to zoom the presentation editor with Ctrl and the mouse wheel, Ctrl+= and Ctrl+- or a zoom menu in the toolbar, and Ctrl+0 fits the whole page back in view.

Notes

  • Ctrl+Z now undoes any action in the notes in order, including moving sections and everything to do with screenshots, and Ctrl+Y redoes it.
  • Dragging sections has been reworked: the blue line now always shows exactly where a section will land, between sections or side by side, and a section no longer needs a separate icon to be moved.
  • Added the ability to select whole sections by dragging across them and to delete them, or copy and paste them as sections, including between spots. A single range can be selected the same way.
  • Range cards can now be dragged and resized in both directions, and a new card has the shape of a real range from the start.
  • Added the ability to crop a range by double-clicking it and to copy its screenshot from the right-click menu.
  • Fixed resizing of sections: a section now always returns to full width, and its edge is much easier to grab.

Library

  • Reanalyse button: a spot, or several at once, can now be analysed again in one click. Settings, notes, tags and solved models are kept, manual corrections are reset, and if the spot cannot be read, it stays unchanged.
  • Anonymity in one click: beside Table, Lobby and All in the spot window there are now three icons, Names shown, Hero name hidden and All names hidden, to switch between them instantly.
  • Added the ability to change any detail that was read wrongly, from Hero's hole cards to the tournament stage.
  • When several spots are selected, the right panel now offers Combine, Reanalyse and Archive buttons.
  • In the spot view, Table and Lobby show the original screenshot when names are not hidden, and Copy image copies only the capture itself, exactly as it is shown.
  • The full-size view now has a switch that shows the capture itself in a light or a dark theme, also on the T key. The choice applies on the spot page and in the spot window too, so one switch sets how the capture looks everywhere.
  • Export to HRC is now also available in the full-size view, so spots can be sent to HRC without leaving it.
  • The library list now shows Hero's hole cards next to each spot, and the Spot list button in the top right corner of the library opens a menu where you choose what each spot's row shows: the dot, hole cards, tags, status and notes, buy-in, effective stack, stage and date.
  • In the grid view, two cards now fit in each row, each showing the table capture with the effective stack, stage, buy-in and date in its corner, so spots are easier to tell apart at a glance.
  • Renaming a spot now also renames it on its picture, so copies and PDFs show the new name too.
  • Getting started and the library tour are now shorter and also cover the new controls, so you can get going faster.

Import

  • One Import button on Home, in the library and in Raw captures now takes screenshots, raw captures and archives of spots, and SpotShot tells which is which by itself.
  • Added the ability to drag a screenshot straight from a viewer or a browser, not only a file, onto any SpotShot window.
  • Ctrl+V with a screenshot now imports it in any window.
  • If imported spots or raw captures are already in SpotShot, it now asks whether to Replace, Skip or Add anyway, as the screenshot import already did.
  • The library list now stays still while spots arrive, after Analyse or an import: new spots slide in at the top with no scrolling or flicker.

Captures

  • If a tournament is in Late reg or the Late stage, the capture now also shows the share of the field left, for example "Late reg, 47% field left".
  • GGPoker tournaments like Day 2 are now read more fully: the share of the field left is counted from all entries, and seats and tickets added to a prize, such as "+ $10K seat", are counted.
  • For rooms whose lobby shows the average mystery bounty, the unnecessary chest breakdown has been removed, along with the extra caption on the capture.
  • SpotShot now finds the CoinPoker lobby even when the table's title does not name the tournament. It also makes the lobby taller, so long prize lists fit in full.

Fixes

  • Export to HRC now sets the model in HRC by itself, so a ChipEV setting there no longer stops the export, and if something is missing, such as a starting stack, SpotShot asks for it before HRC opens.
  • Screenshots in the spot view are now displayed noticeably sharper.
  • Fixed a problem with tournament names: they are now kept in full, for example "#18: Turbo - Bounty Closer". ACR no longer loses its guarantee, and "guaranteed" now shows as GTD.
  • Fixed cases in which saved HRC models with names like v1 and v2 were mistakenly linked to other spots.
  • Fixed cases in which a lobby imported by an older version was saved as a table: Reanalyse now turns it into a Structure only spot without errors.
  • Fixed a rare crash during analysis or import.
  • Fixed cases in which a PokerStars dealer button partly hidden by a badge was missed, a bet was credited to a player who had already folded, an ACR player's name showed as their last action, and GGPoker prize lists in Russian, with yen and "+ Bounty" rows, were not read for Structure only spots.
  • Fixed a problem in which the state of captures in the queue could be lost if another program was using the queue file at that moment.
  • Fixed cases in which a reanalysed spot kept its old place under Newest analysed first.
  • Fixed a problem in which the card shown after a capture had a title bar on newer versions of Windows.
  • Fixed a problem in which deleting several raw captures at once ignored the Ask before deleting setting.
  • Pop-up windows are now solid and easy to read.
  • Screenshots of hands from PokerCraft and the client's replayer are now recognized far more reliably, including ones compressed when sent through messengers.
  • Fixed the ante calculation: it is now counted per player, and for hands saved in BB the blinds are taken from the tournament's lobby.
  • If one or more players are knocked out in a hand, their bounties are now worked out from what was added to the winner's bounty.
  • The level, blinds, ante, average stack and prize pool are now read more reliably from Russian GGPoker lobbies, including when a lobby is imported on its own.
  • Improved the app's security: updates are now checked before they are opened, and archives imported from other people are checked much more strictly.
  • Other small fixes in the library and presentations.

1.0.3

1 Nov 2026

Overview

One-click presentations from your HRC trees, manual spot correction, import of spots from GGMillion$ streams and a round of fixes.

HRC presentations

  • Presentations in one click: pick an HRC tree saved to a spot, press Create presentation, and its ranges and bubble factors are packaged nicely in a PDF presentation.
  • Smart range selector: SpotShot goes deep into the tree to get all required ranges.
  • Smart layouts: each new presentation picks its layout from the spot's action.
  • Spot-specific pay jumps sit under the table along with bubble factors.

Library

  • Spot import now supports screenshots from English and Russian GGMillion$ livestreams.
  • Fix a misread spot in seconds: right-click a seat or use the pencil to change a position, a stack or a bounty, or to add or remove a player.
  • Ability to manually correct the tournament info and the payouts in the library spot view.
  • Paste screenshots straight into the library with Ctrl+V: a table and its lobby become one spot, and Combine joins a table spot with a structure only spot.
  • Full-size view: cycle through your spots with the arrows and make decisions on whether to keep or delete a spot as you go.
  • Drop spot archives on the library to import them, several at once.
  • Spots with notes show a notes icon, and ITM, Bubble and FT are now the spot's stage instead of tags.
  • Stage unknown from a PokerCraft import? After pressing Export to HRC, a pop-up window opens for you to enter missing tournament details for a smooth HRC tree construction.

Fixes

  • Screenshots from GGPoker client and PokerCraft captures can now be matched with one another.
  • Bounties are now read from PokerCraft hand pictures.
  • PokerOK screenshots in Russian, GGPoker lobbies and ACR tables are read better and more reliably.
  • CoinPoker lobbies are accepted again after the recent CoinPoker update.
  • PokerDom tables are no longer captured black.
  • Fixed a bug where anonymous mode could cover the tournament info on the table.
  • Many smaller fixes in the library and presentations.

1.0.2

22 Oct 2026

Overview

Export to HRC, screenshot import for every room, GGPoker through your own screenshots, and a new look.

Export to HRC

  • One button sends a spot to HoldemResources Calculator, and Open in HRC loads its tree.
  • HRC trees saved under the spot's Capture id are attached to the spot automatically, multiple trees can be saved for one spot.
  • Mystery bounty tournaments: the average mystery bounty is automatically calculated and used as the knockout in HRC.
  • Every HRC file on a spot has its own Open in HRC button, and Detach puts the file back where it came from.
  • Setting "Do not move HRC files to the library": HRC files stay where they are and are linked to the spot from there.

GGPoker

  • SpotShot no longer interacts with GG Network clients (GGPoker, PokerOK, Natural8): it does not capture, open, click or move their windows.
  • SpotShot can stay open beside a GG client and keeps working with your other rooms; analysis still waits until every poker client, GG included, is closed.
  • For GG spots, take screenshots of the table and the lobby yourself, or save the hand replay and the tournament result from PokerCraft, and import them after your session. PokerCraft pictures are paired by the tournament name.
  • A step-by-step guide for GGPoker spots at spotshot.gg/ggpoker

Import your screenshots

  • Import your own screenshots in every room: press Import screenshots on the main window or in the library and choose the new screenshots, a folder or files, or drop them on either window.
  • SpotShot matches tables to lobbies automatically. It recognises the room of each screenshot and pairs each table with its lobby by the tournament, the players left and the time.
  • Choose a single folder and SpotShot watches the folder on every startup to see if any new screenshots are made and ready to be imported.
  • A table screenshot alone gives the hand setup; a lobby screenshot alone gives the tournament structure, shown in the library as Structure only.
  • Imported screenshots are ready to be analysed as spots in the library under Raw Captures tab after your session.

New look

  • A new look for the main window, the library and the spot windows
  • New custom theme settings in the native Windows 11 style.
  • SpotShot runs and starts much faster, and the library opens faster.

Library

  • Every spot now shows its tournament stage: Late reg, Near bubble, Bubble, Stone bubble, ITM, Pay jump, Pre FT or FT, and you can filter and search by it.
  • Newest first: Raw captures lists the newest captures first and All spots the newest analysed first; either can be sorted by table time instead, and the choice is remembered.
  • Each spot keeps its original screenshots and a reader log, so a misread spot can be sent with what caused it. A spot archive includes them only when you tick the bug report box.
  • Your own colours for tags and folders.
  • Library header reworked: Sort and View menus, Reviewed in the sidebar, clicking a status again clears it.
  • An open that leaves less than half the stack behind is now named "RFI Ai".
  • A capture taken after your own raise is named for the spot you faced.
  • If a table and lobby were incorrectly matched, you can replace or add the correct table/lobby image to the spot manually. The spot is reanalysed automatically after the changes.

Fixes

  • A HUD covering the lobby button no longer stops the lobby capture, and if it still does, SpotShot says which program to move.
  • Winamax: another table of the same tournament is no longer captured as the lobby.
  • GGPoker Ocean KO: everything is read except the knockouts (Ocean KO not yet fully supported).
  • Anonymous mode covers names more precisely on PokerStars.
  • Hand reading improved in every room: hero's cards, bets, all-ins, seats and players at short-handed tables.
  • The library no longer slows down after zooming a capture, and recovers by itself if its window freezes.
  • Lobbies are captured slightly faster across all poker rooms.
  • Fixed connection issue on networks that use IPv6.
  • Improved security in verifying each of the new updates.
  • Many other small fixes in the interface, the capture and the hand reading.
  • SpotShot uses much less memory during a session and after an analysis run.
  • ACR: the bounty badges on every seat are read, and the prize list that scrolls sideways is captured to its last column.

1.0.0

1 Oct 2026

Overview

The first official release of SpotShot: screenshots, hand setups and tournament structures, with a spot library for your ICM spots on Windows.

SpotShot features

  • One key, the whole spot. Press one key at the table. SpotShot captures the table, opens the lobby and captures it with the payouts automatically, then combines the screenshots into one capture.
  • Hand setup and tournament structure for each spot. Once your session is over and poker clients are closed, SpotShot analyses every capture on your PC and generates a hand setup .txt file, ready to import to HRC and GTO Wizard, and a tournament structure .json file for HRC.
  • Every figure worked out: player knockouts in BB, total chips in play and the average mystery bounty are calculated automatically.
  • Anonymous mode: an option to hide your and other players' names on the capture during the spot analysis.
  • A spot library: every spot described and sorted, found by position, stack, stage, hole cards, room or custom tag, with notes and a one-page PDF presentation editor for each spot. Send a spot, a search or the whole library to your friend or coach in one .zip.
  • Works with GGPoker, PokerStars, PartyPoker, 888poker, ACR, Winamax, WPT Global, CoinPoker, iPoker (Red Star), PokerDom, Chico and QQPK.

SpotShot design

  • Private by design. While you play, SpotShot only takes screenshots: nothing is analysed, and the spot library stays unavailable, until every poker client is closed. No account needed, player names do not appear in the hand setup files, and your captures never leave your PC.
  • Security. During your session SpotShot acts as a regular screenshot app: apart from taking screenshots, it only opens the lobby of the chosen table and gets its payouts or bounties on screen.
  • SpotShot goes online only to check for updates and for its periodic access check; all the analysis is done offline on your PC.
